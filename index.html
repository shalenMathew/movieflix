MOVIEFLIX

MovieFlix is an ad-free, open-source journal for tracking your favourite movies and TV shows offline.

Shalen Mathew

Developer

Features

Watchlist & Favorites

Keep your next watch close, and save the movies and shows you love.

Series tracking

Pick up each series where you left off.

Share posters

Turn your movie picks into posters worth sharing.

Lists & list sharing

Organize movies into custom lists and share them as poster images.

Schedule reminders

Choose when you want to watch, and get a reminder at the right time.

Personal notes

Save thoughts, reactions, and details alongside your favorite movies and shows.

Media gallery

Keep favorite scenes, fan art, and other images together in one place.
